# Initial Concept
LokiGame is an online live-service backend for a server-authoritative idle RPG. It features zero-player gameplay where battles are simulated deterministically, idle progression via "Math-on-Read" evaluation, and a unique gacha system where every hero is a unique combination of stats and visuals.

# Product Guide - LokiGame

## Target Audience
The game is specifically designed for **Hardcore Theorycrafters**. These are players who find joy in deep stat optimization, analyzing complex mechanics, and perfecting team compositions to overcome difficult challenges.

## Core Goals & Experience
*   **Deep Customization:** Every hero summoned is unique. Through a combination of rolled stats and diverse visual identities, players feel a strong sense of ownership and variety in their roster.
*   **Strategic Planning:** Success is earned through brainpower. Victory depends on understanding counter-mechanics and synergy within team compositions rather than simply out-leveling content.
*   **Transparent Mechanics:** To support theorycrafting, the game provides clear data and detailed logs, allowing players to analyze exactly why a battle was won or lost.

## Game World & Theme
LokiGame is set in a **High-Fantasy Classic** universe. Players will recruit knights, mages, and other iconic archetypes to explore a world filled with magic and ancient mystery, rendered with a polished and epic aesthetic.

## Functional Scope: Progression Systems
*   **Procedural Rifts:** To keep the strategy fresh, players will face randomly generated challenges that require them to adapt their teams to changing enemy types and environmental modifiers.
*   **Infinite Tower:** For the ultimate test of optimization, an endless scaling tower allows players to push their best teams to their absolute limits, competing for prestige and progress.
