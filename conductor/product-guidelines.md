# Product Guidelines - LokiGame

## Communication & Tone
*   **Analytical & Precise:** All technical communication, battle logs, and stat descriptions must prioritize mathematical accuracy and clarity. The goal is to provide theorycrafters with the raw data they need to optimize their strategies. Use standardized terminology for all mechanics.
*   **Grim & Desperate Lore:** While the logs are technical, the narrative context is somber. The world is on the brink of collapse, and every summoned hero is a precious, fleeting resource in a desperate struggle.

## Visual Identity & Design
*   **Visual Splendor:** The game's presentation should feature high-quality artwork and impactful animations. Even though it is a "Zero-Player" game, the visualization of the simulation must feel epic and rewarding. 
*   **Functional Contrast:** Technical data overlays should feel distinct from the lush, high-fantasy art, perhaps using a "magical interface" aesthetic that blends precision with the game's theme.

## User Experience (UX)
*   **Impactful Decisions:** UX design should focus on the "Preparation Phase." Navigating menus to build teams, optimize gear, and analyze logs should feel meaningful. Avoid frequent, low-value interactions (clicks for the sake of clicking).
*   **Strategic Depth:** The interface must support complex decision-making, providing tools for comparing heroes, simulating potential outcomes, and tracking long-term progression.
